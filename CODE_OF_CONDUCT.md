# Code of conduct

Everyone working on or discussing this project is expected to:

- be respectful and constructive, and assume good intent;
- give and accept honest technical feedback, especially about risk and safety;
- never pressure anyone to weaken a safety control or to share credentials;
- keep private information private (keys, account details, balances, personal data).

Harassment, discrimination, or abusive behaviour of any kind is not tolerated. The project
owner may remove comments, revoke access, or take other action in response.

Report problems privately to the repository owner through GitHub. This policy is inspired by
the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
