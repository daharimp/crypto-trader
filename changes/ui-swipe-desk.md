### Changed
- The dashboard is now four sections you slide between (Overview, Positions, Trades,
  Markets) by swiping, with the arrow keys, or by tapping a name, on a phone and a computer
  alike (ADR-037, replacing the ADR-030 drag-and-resize cards). It opens on the leverage
  paper account and has a switch for the spot account.
- Positions show leverage, the owner's money in each, gain or loss in dollars, the
  liquidation price and how far away it is; tapping one slides its details in. Trades list
  every closed trade with dollars won or lost and mark liquidations.
- The page uses the crypto-trader design system, light by default, with an Appearance
  choice: Auto, Light, Dark, or Day and night. Its three fonts are bundled (SIL Open Font
  License 1.1), so the page still loads nothing from another host.
