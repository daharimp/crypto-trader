# EXP-2026-002: `sma_trend` on real Kraken data (120 days of 4h candles)

- **Date run:** 2026-11-02 (UTC 23:50). **Approved by:** owner (option 1, "real-data test").
- **Status:** completed. **Verdict:** no evidence of an edge. Beaten by buy-and-hold on raw
  and risk-adjusted terms. Fails the UD-21 success target.

## Question

Does `sma_trend` 1.0.0 (SMA 20/50 cross, owner-approved Balanced risk settings) add value
over simply holding the same coins, after realistic costs, on real Kraken prices?

## Data

Fetched with `trader data fetch --exchange kraken` (public OHLC). Kraken's API serves only the
latest 720 candles per timeframe, so this is **about 120 days of 4h candles** (2026-07-05 to
2026-11-02) plus 720 daily candles (from 2024-11-12) for eligibility.

All datasets passed DQ-01 to DQ-11. Kraken's window moves forward, so these IDs identify
the exact candles used. A re-fetch later produces different datasets.

## Method

- Shipped configs (profile v4, risk v2, eligibility v3); no experiment overrides.
- Evaluation from 2026-07-15 (after the 51-bar indicator warm-up) to 2026-11-02, about 110 days.
  Strategy parameters are fixed (R-09), so the whole window is out-of-sample.
- Eligibility from daily candles (`--eligibility-datasets`), matching live sessions.
- Grid: capital $250 (the approved live cap) and $2,500 × costs low (0.10% fee, 5 bps),
  base (0.40%, 10 bps), stress (0.60%, 25 bps), per side. Base-cost $250 also split into halves.
- Benchmarks: cash, buy-and-hold BTC and ETH, and an equal-weight buy-and-hold of all five
  coins (`bm_ew_universe`), with the same costs.

## Results

| Run | Strategy | Max DD | Trades (win %) | Fees | Hold all 5 | Hold BTC |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| $250, low costs | **+7.8%** | 8.7% | 35 (26%) | $3.01 | +16.2% | +18.3% |
| $250, base costs | **+3.4%** | 11.5% | 35 (23%) | $11.80 | +15.8% | +17.9% |
| $250, stress costs | **+1.4%** | 11.9% | 35 (20%) | $15.94 | +15.6% | +17.7% |
| $2,500 (any cost) | same % as $250 | | | 10× | | |
| $250 base, first half (Jul 15–Sep 8) | +11.1% | 5.2% | 15 | $5.26 | +23.4% | +21.2% |
| $250 base, second half (Sep 8–Nov 2) | **−6.1%** | 8.8% | 17 | $6.47 | −6.1% | −3.2% |

Risk-adjusted (base costs, full window):

| Metric | Strategy | Hold all 5 | Hold BTC |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sharpe-like | 0.60 | 1.29 | 1.80 |
| Sortino-like | 1.27 | 2.21 | 3.33 |
| Calmar | 1.00 | 2.94 | 6.30 |
| Volatility (annualized) | 22% | 53% | 37% |
| Max drawdown | 11.5% | 21.2% | 11.4% |

Other observations:

- **Fees matter most.** At base costs the bot paid $11.80 on $250 (4.7% of capital), more
  than its $8.50 gain. Lower fees more than doubled the return.
- **Small capital is not a problem in itself.** $250 and $2,500 gave identical percentages:
  no orders fell below Kraken's minimums.
- **ETH was ineligible for about half the window** (30-day median volume below the $50M Core
  threshold), as predicted. It was also the only asset that lost money (−$8.58).
- Average exposure was about 21% and average holding time about 4 days. Profit factor 1.22,
  win rate 23%: a few winners carried many small losses, which is typical of trend following.
- Every run's journal verified, and every order was traceable.

## Verdict against UD-21 (approved: relative risk-adjusted targets plus consistency)

- **Beats the benchmarks on risk-adjusted terms: no.** Sharpe-like, Sortino-like, and
  Calmar are all well below holding the five coins and holding BTC.
- **Drawdown inside the breaker (15%): yes** (11.5% to 11.9%), but no better than holding BTC.
- **Consistency: no.** It gained in the rising half but lost as much as the basket in the
  falling half, so it did not protect capital in the down move.

**Interpretation.** About 110 days, one market phase, and 35 trades are far too little
evidence to call the strategy good or bad. Nothing here supports it, and nothing suggests
it beats doing nothing clever. Going live with this strategy now would be a bet, not a
tested decision.

## Recommended next steps (for the owner to approve or deny)

1. Keep live disabled. Run paper on the server (the 42-cycle gate) to test operations, not
   to prove the strategy.
2. Get Kraken's full downloadable history (years of 4h candles) and repeat this test with
   walk-forward splits (BL-025, BL-029). This is the evidence that matters.
3. Add the comparison strategies (BL-022), including a random-entry control. An edge has
   to beat a random strategy with the same risk rules.
4. Cut costs: limit (maker) orders instead of market orders, and check the account's real
   Kraken fee tier. Costs alone decided the difference between +1.4% and +7.8% here.

## Reproduction

```bash
trader data fetch --exchange kraken --symbol BTC/USD --instrument BTC-USD --timeframe 4h \
  --start <now-125d> --end <now>          # and --timeframe 1d --start <now-740d>; all 5 coins
trader backtest run --profile configs/profiles/balanced-paper.yaml \
  --datasets <five 4h IDs> --eligibility-datasets <five 1d IDs> \
  --strategy sma_trend --timeframe 4h --cost-scenario base --start 2026-07-15T00:00:00Z
```

The $250 runs used a scratch copy of the configs with `paper_capital_usd: 250`.
