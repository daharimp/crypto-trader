# EXP-2026-013 plan: does trading both directions beat long-only? (pre-registered)

Written and committed **before** any result was computed. Every trial below is reported,
including the ones that look bad. Research only: shorts can run only in the paper-only perps
package (ADR-025); real-money shorting is not authorized by anything here.

## Question

The owner asked for strategies that ride downtrends as well as uptrends. Does adding the short
side to established trend-following rules add money after realistic costs, compared with the
same rule long-only and with buy-and-hold?

## Why these rules (published, not invented)

- **Time-series momentum (TSMOM)**: Moskowitz, Ooi & Pedersen (2012, *JFE*): long if the
  trailing return is positive, short if negative, each position scaled to 40% annual volatility.
- **Blend of 1-, 3- and 12-month TSMOM**: Hurst, Ooi & Pedersen (2017, *JPM*), "A Century of
  Evidence on Trend-Following Investing".
- **1-month TSMOM alone**: crypto studies report return persistence over roughly 1 to 8 weeks.
- **Volatility scaling on/off**: Kim, Tse & Wald (2016, *JFM*) find the scaling, not the trend
  signal, drives much of TSMOM's result, so both versions are tested.
- Prior evidence is mixed: some crypto studies find long-short portfolios do worse than
  long-only. This test does not assume either answer.

## Trials (8 per coin, fixed in advance)

| ID | Signal | Sides | Volatility scaling |
|---|---|---|---|
| T1 | TSMOM blend 1/3/12 months | long + short | none (full size) |
| T2 | TSMOM blend 1/3/12 months | long only | none |
| T3 | TSMOM blend 1/3/12 months | long + short | 40% target, never above 1x |
| T4 | TSMOM blend 1/3/12 months | long only | 40% target, never above 1x |
| T5 | TSMOM 1 month | long + short | none |
| T6 | TSMOM 1 month | long only | none |
| T7 | TSMOM 1 month | long + short | 40% target, never above 1x |
| T8 | TSMOM 1 month | long only | 40% target, never above 1x |

Reference: buy-and-hold. Months are 30 days (180 four-hour bars); 12 months = 2,190 bars.
No parameter is tuned; no leverage above 1x.

## Data

Coinbase public 4h candles: BTC-USD (~10.7 years), ETH-USD (~10 years), SOL-USD (~5.5 years),
via `scripts/fetch_coinbase_history.py`. The first 12 months of each coin are warm-up.

## Costs

- **Primary (decides the answer):** owner-approved Jupiter perps values (UD-23 v5): 7 bps per
  side plus borrow 0.01% per hour on any open position, long or short.
- **Optimistic bound (context only):** 7 bps per side, no borrow. True costs on other venues
  (e.g. Kraken US margin rollover, perps funding) fall between the two.

## Measures (dollars, on a constant $2,500)

- Average dollars gained or lost per year, and the worst single calendar year.
- Largest peak-to-trough drop.
- Calendar years split into up years and down years for the coin, to show what the short side
  does in bear markets.
- **90% range by block bootstrap** (30-day blocks, 2,000 resamples) for the yearly result and
  for the difference "long+short minus long-only".

## Decision rule (fixed in advance)

The short side "adds money" only if, at **primary** costs, the 90% range of
(long+short minus long-only) is above $0 per year for the same rule on **at least 2 of 3
coins**. Otherwise the answer is "not shown", and the forward-paper candidate stays long-only.
A rule that passes goes to forward paper trading in the perps package, never straight to money.

## Implementation details fixed before running (added with the script, still before results)

- Volatility for scaling: realized volatility over the trailing 60 days (360 bars), close to
  the ~60-day centre of mass in Moskowitz et al. (2012); scale rounded to quarter steps (lab).
- Positions decided at a bar's close fill at the next bar's open (lab, ADR-027).
