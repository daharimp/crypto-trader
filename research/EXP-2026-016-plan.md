# EXP-2026-016 plan: does the consensus signal deserve to stay the default? (pre-registered)

Written and committed **before** any result was computed. Every row below is reported,
including the ones that look bad. Research only: nothing here changes a config value or the
paper session; the owner decides. Paper perps only (ADR-025, ADR-031); nothing here
authorizes real-money margin or perps (UD-24).

## Question (owner decision UD-29, 2026-11-12, relayed by the main session; ADR-035)

The paper session's default signal is now `consensus_sma_tsmom`: long or short only when
`sma_stance` 20/50 and the sign of the 30-day return agree, otherwise flat. It rests on an
exploratory check that was not pre-registered. Does it do better than each rule alone?

## Signals (exactly as in `trader.perps.signals` on `feat/perps-consensus-signal`)

- **SMA:** long while the 20-bar average is above the 50-bar average, short while below.
- **TSMOM:** long if the close is above its level 30 days (180 bars) ago, short if below.
- **CONSENSUS:** the shared side when SMA and TSMOM agree; flat when they disagree.

## Position model, leverage, and costs (unchanged from EXP-2026-015, ADR-034)

One coin at a time on a **constant $2,500**: $250 collateral per trade, no compounding;
decide at a close, fill at the next open; 7 bps fee per side on size; 20 bps maintenance;
liquidation checked against each bar's adverse extreme with the whole collateral lost.
Opening leverage is the paper session's approved rule (ADR-032, UD-26, UD-27):
`1 / (4 x 30-day typical daily move + closing fee + maintenance)`, rounded down to 0.1,
between 1x and 25x, fixed for the life of the trade.

- **Primary holding cost:** measured hourly funding (ADR-033).
- **Context only:** the paper session's approved borrow fee of 0.0016% per hour charged on
  size to both sides (UD-28, perps config v9), because that is what the paper session shows.

## Two panels (fixed now)

- **Panel A, the requested history:** BTC, ETH, SOL with Coinbase 4h prices and Deribit
  funding, on the EXP-2026-014/015 windows (BTC and ETH from 2019-04-30, SOL from
  2022-06-15). Both single rules have already been looked at on these coins and dates
  (EXP-2026-014, EXP-2026-015) and the consensus was explored on recent history by the main
  session, so Panel A is **not unseen data**.
- **Panel B, coins never looked at in this project:** DOGE, LTC, LINK, AVAX, ADA with
  Coinbase 4h prices from 2023-03-01 and Hyperliquid hourly funding, each from its first 4h
  bar fully covered by funding (about May 2023; ADA about October 2023). Chosen before any
  result from the coins that have both a Coinbase USD market over the whole period and
  Hyperliquid funding; XRP is excluded because Coinbase had no XRP market in early 2023.
  Only coverage dates were checked when this was written.

A 4h bar with a missing funding hour is charged the hours that exist (a position's path
cannot skip a bar); the count is reported.

## Measures (dollars, on a constant $2,500)

Per signal and coin: average dollars per year with a 90% range (circular block bootstrap,
30-day blocks, 2,000 resamples, fixed seed), worst calendar year, largest peak-to-trough drop
in dollars, trades, liquidations, share of time in a position, average opening leverage, and
funding dollars per year. Paired differences with 90% ranges: CONSENSUS minus SMA and
CONSENSUS minus TSMOM. Descriptive only: dollars per year per dollar of largest drop.

## Decision rules (fixed in advance; primary cost)

- **"Consensus beats rule X"** only if the 90% range of (CONSENSUS minus X) is above $0 per
  year on at least 2 of 3 Panel A coins. **"Rule X beats consensus"** if it is below $0 on at
  least 2 of 3.
- **Supported as the default** only if all three hold: (1) CONSENSUS's own 90% range is above
  $0 on at least 2 of 3 Panel A coins; (2) neither single rule beats it on Panel A; (3) on
  Panel B, CONSENSUS's average dollars per year is at least as high as each single rule's on
  at least 3 of 5 coins, and its own 90% range is above $0 on at least 3 of 5.
- **Contradicted** if a single rule beats it on Panel A, or if on Panel B its average dollars
  per year is below a single rule's on at least 4 of 5 coins.
- Otherwise: **not settled**. The consensus is then neither shown better nor shown worse, and
  whether it stays the default is the owner's call; the forward paper session is the test.

## Known limits (stated before running)

- Panel A is not unseen data (see above), so its dollar levels are optimistic. Panel B is
  unseen but short (about 3.4 years, one market cycle) and its five coins move together, so
  they are not five independent tests.
- Coinbase spot highs and lows stand in for perps wicks; Deribit and Hyperliquid funding
  stand in for the venue; Jupiter's borrow fee appears only as the context row.
- One coin at a time on constant capital: account-level limits (three positions, 400% total
  size, the daily-loss halt), compounding, and running out of money are not modelled.
- No parameter is tuned. No result here is a forecast or a promise of profit.
