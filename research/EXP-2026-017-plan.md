# EXP-2026-017 plan: does switching style by market regime beat the 1-month trend rule? (pre-registered)

Written and committed **before** any result was computed. Every row below is reported,
including the ones that look bad. Research only: nothing here changes a config value or the
running paper session; the owner decides. Paper perps only (ADR-025, ADR-031).

## Question (owner request, 2026-11-15)

The owner wants a strategy that trades uptrends and downtrends and also copes with sideways
markets. The paper session's rule, `tsmom_30d` (UD-30), follows the 30-day trend and is always
in the market, so it is exposed to choppy, sideways stretches. Does a rule that **switches
style by regime** (trend-following when the market is trending, buying dips and selling
rallies when it is ranging) earn more than `tsmom_30d`? The owner's yardstick is **most
profit** (dollars per year); drops and liquidations are reported beside it.

## Signals (fixed now; +1 long, -1 short, 0 flat, decided at a 4h close)

- **TSMOM (baseline):** sign of the return over the last 30 days (180 bars), exactly as
  `tsmom_30d`.
- **Regime measure:** efficiency ratio over 30 days,
  `ER = |close - close 180 bars ago| / sum of |bar-to-bar close changes| over those 180 bars`
  (1 = price moved in a straight line, near 0 = it went back and forth). The market counts as
  **trending** when ER is above its own median over the previous 365 days (2,190 bars, past
  data only), otherwise **ranging**. A relative cut avoids choosing an absolute number.
- **MEANREV (component, context):** `z = (close - 20-bar average) / 20-bar standard deviation`;
  short when `z > 1`, long when `z < -1`, flat otherwise.
- **REGIME (candidate):** TSMOM's side while trending, MEANREV's side while ranging.

No parameter above may change after results are seen. Variants, if any, go in a new plan.

## Position model, leverage, and costs (unchanged from EXP-2026-016)

One coin at a time on a **constant $2,500**: $250 collateral per trade, no compounding;
decide at a close, fill at the next open; 7 bps fee per side on size; 20 bps maintenance;
liquidation against each bar's adverse extreme with the whole collateral lost. Opening
leverage is the paper session's approved rule (ADR-032, UD-26, UD-27): 4 typical 30-day daily
moves of buffer, rounded down to 0.1x, between 1x and 25x, fixed for the life of a trade.
Engine: `trader.perps.leverage_replay` (ADR-034), as in EXP-2026-015/016.

- **Primary holding cost:** measured hourly funding (ADR-033).
- **Context only:** the paper session's approved borrow fee, 0.0016% per hour (UD-28).

## Panels and window (fixed now)

- **Panel A:** BTC, ETH, SOL, Coinbase 4h prices, Deribit funding (seen before in
  EXP-2026-014 to 016, so **not unseen data**).
- **Panel B:** DOGE, LTC, LINK, AVAX, ADA, Coinbase 4h prices, Hyperliquid funding (used once,
  in EXP-2026-016, for other rules; never for REGIME or MEANREV).
- **Warm-up:** 2,190 + 180 + 1 bars on every coin (the regime measure's longest look-back), so
  every rule is scored on the same bars. The window ends at the last bar with complete funding.

## Measures

Per coin and rule: dollars per year with a 90% range (circular block bootstrap, 30-day
blocks, 2,000 resamples, seed 17), worst calendar year, largest drop, trades, liquidations,
share of time in the market, average leverage, funding per year. Paired difference REGIME
minus TSMOM with its 90% range. Share of bars spent trending.

## Decision rule (primary cost; fixed now)

- **Supported** (REGIME earns more than TSMOM): the 90% range of REGIME minus TSMOM is above
  $0 on at least 2 of 3 Panel A coins, **and** REGIME's average is at least TSMOM's on at
  least 3 of 5 Panel B coins.
- **Contradicted:** that range is below $0 on at least 2 of 3 Panel A coins, **or** REGIME's
  average is below TSMOM's on at least 4 of 5 Panel B coins.
- Otherwise **not settled**.

Whatever the verdict, it is a statement about past data under these costs, not a forecast.
A supported REGIME would next run as a **second paper account** beside the current one; it
would not replace the running 7-day test or touch any approved value without the owner.
