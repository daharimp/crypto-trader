# EXP-2026-022 plan: stop-loss and take-profit ladder for every bot

Written 2026-11-22, before any result on real data was computed. Research only; changes no
configuration.

## Question
Owner (2026-11-22): every trade must always have a stop, and the stop must move to protect
gains as the trade works: "hits 1st TP > move SL, hits 2nd TP > move SL > 3rd TP". Which
stop and ladder placement works best for each bot's own entries?

## Evidence base (why these candidates)
- Kaminski & Lo (2014), "When do stop-loss rules stop losses?": stops add value when returns
  have momentum and cost return under a random walk. All three bots are trend followers, so
  stops are expected to help.
- Han, Zhou & Zhu (2016), "Taming momentum crashes": a simple stop on momentum positions cut
  the worst monthly loss sharply and raised the Sharpe ratio.
- Clare, Seaton, Smith & Thomas (2013), "Breaking into the blackbox": trailing stops on trend
  following improved risk-adjusted returns.
- Practitioner standards: volatility (ATR) stops, LeBeau's chandelier exit (3 ATR from the
  best price), breakeven moves, and scaled exits (partial take-profits).

## Data
Binance spot candles, BTC/ETH/SOL, 2020-01 (SOL 2020-08) to 2026-11-21, 4h and 1h.
In-sample: before 2024-01-01. Holdout: 2024-01-01 onward.

## Method grid (fixed in research/stop_ladder_replay.py)
No stop; each bot's current stop; fixed stops of 1, 1.5, 2, 3 daily moves, each with and
without a breakeven move at +1R; chandelier trails at 2, 3, 4 daily moves; the owner's ladder
with an initial stop of 1.5 or 2 daily moves, TP sets 1/2/3, 1/2/4, 1.5/3/5, 2/4/6 R, splits
33/33/34, 50/25/25, 25/25/50, and either a TP3 cap or a runner that trails 3 daily moves
after TP2. TP1 moves the stop to breakeven (fees covered), TP2 moves it to TP1.

## Selection rule
Rank by in-sample MAR (return per year / max drawdown). The recommendation is the method
that ranks well in-sample for all three bots AND does not lose to "no stop" or to the
current stop in the holdout. Holdout numbers are reported for every method; nothing is
re-tuned on the holdout.

## 6. Added after the first results (exploratory, labelled "x:")
The capped ladders lost most of the return. Before concluding, uncapped variants were added
(stop ratchets every 1/2/3 R with nothing sold; TP1/TP2 partials with the rest held to the
signal exit, with and without further ratchets) and a check that the next-UTC-day wait after
an exit is not the cause. These were not pre-registered and are reported as exploratory.
