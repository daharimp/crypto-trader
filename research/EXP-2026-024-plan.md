# EXP-2026-024 plan: fresh-data test of the fixes, and a top-20 faster-trading account

Written 2026-11-26 01:45 UTC and committed before any of the new candle files were copied
into the research machine. Research only; changes no configuration.

## Why
Owner, 2026-11-26: backtest the fixes first on data the bots were never tuned on, run the
winners as a new paper bot beside the existing ones (existing runs untouched), and "while
testing on paper I want more frequent trades on more of the top cryptos, just choose the
top 20, we aren't pushing our testing enough".

EXP-2026-023 (BTC, ETH, SOL, 2020-10 to 2026-11) found, for the leveraged accounts:
- the current settings swing hard (backtest max drawdown 57-74%) and the opening stops are
  the largest single cost, four to five times the whole fee bill;
- a wider volatility stop at the same loss per stop (liquidation buffer 8 instead of 4,
  i.e. a stop about 2.4 typical daily moves away instead of 1.2, about half the leverage)
  cut stop-outs from 26-29% of entries to 6-7% with a similar Sharpe;
- no signal variant beat the live signal outside noise; the one that won on 2020-2023 was
  the worst on 2024-2026;
- same-side risk caps gave mixed results that depend on small implementation choices.

The 2024+ period has now been looked at by about 22 experiments, so it is a validation set,
not a clean test. This plan fixes what will be run on data that no experiment has used.

## Harness
`research/exp024_lib.py`: the real paper account and live signal on any list of coins
(checked to reproduce `exp023_lib` to the cent on BTC, ETH, SOL). Costs as in the live
configs (7 bps a side, borrow 0.0016% an hour); the cost test of EXP-023 says the edge is
gone at about twice these costs, so every table also shows 2x costs.

## Part A: do the fixes hold on fresh three-coin and ten-coin history? (F1, F2)
Settings, everything else as `configs/perps/jupiter.yaml` (ladder kept, 30% stop):
1. LIVE: collateral 10%, buffer 4.
2. HALF: collateral 5%, buffer 4 (same exposure as WIDE, tight stop: the size-matched control).
3. WIDE: collateral 10%, buffer 8, leverage cap 5x.
4. WIDE10: collateral 10%, buffer 10, leverage cap 5x.
5. WIDE+CAP6: WIDE with a 6% same-side open-risk cap.
6. WIDE+CAP3: WIDE with a 3% cap.
For F2 (ten coins) collateral is 3% instead of 10% (HALF 1.5%), `max_open_positions` 10.

WIDE "holds" on a slice if its max drawdown is lower than LIVE's and its Sharpe is not more
than 0.1 lower. It must also beat HALF on Sharpe to show the wider stop helps beyond
simply being smaller. The fix is confirmed if WIDE holds on F1 and F2.

## Part B: a top-20 account (F3, F4, and all 20)
4h decisions, live signal, `max_open_positions` 20, ladder kept, 30% stop. Grid:
collateral per position 1%, 1.5%, 2.5% (loss at a stop 0.3%, 0.45%, 0.75% of equity; 6%,
9%, 15% if all twenty stop together) x buffer 4, 8 (leverage cap 5x with buffer 8) x
same-side cap none, 6%. Twelve settings.
The central setting, fixed now, is collateral 1.5%, buffer 8, no cap: the same 9% at risk
when everything lines up as main has today.

## Part C: more frequent trades
At the central setting, three ways of trading more often:
- T1: the 4h account of Part B (twenty coins instead of three).
- T2: hourly decisions and stops with the trend read from 4h candles (how degen runs).
- T3: the same rule read from hourly candles (50 and 200 hours, the range switch on 180
  hours), hourly decisions.
Reported for each: entries a month, decision clusters a month (coins opened the same UTC
day on the same side count once), return, Sharpe, drawdown, fees as % of equity a year,
at 1x and 2x costs. 15-minute bars are not tested here; they are tested only if T3 keeps
a positive return after 2x costs.

## Selection rule
- A setting goes into a new paper account only if its return after 1x costs is positive on
  the fresh slices it was run on; among those, the one with the best Sharpe on F3 whose
  neighbours in the grid agree. If nothing faster than T1 passes, T1 is recommended and the
  fastest variant is offered to the owner as a labelled stress test, not as a candidate.
- Nothing is re-tuned after seeing these results. Anything added later is labelled
  exploratory.
- All risk values are the owner's to approve. The true test is the forward paper run.
