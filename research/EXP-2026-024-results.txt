EXP-2026-024: fresh-data test of the fixes, a top-20 account, and faster trading
================================================================================
Date: 2026-11-26. Research only; changes no configuration. Plan (committed before the
new candle files were read): EXP-2026-024-plan.md. Run: research/run_exp024.py.
Simulated results on historical data with modelled costs. Not a forecast; profit is not
guaranteed or implied.

Columns: CAGR, Sharpe (daily, 365 days), max drawdown, worst day, entries a month,
decisions a month (coins opened on the same UTC day on the same side count once), share of
entries closed by the opening stop, fees + borrow a year as % of average equity; then
CAGR, Sharpe and max drawdown at twice the fees and twice the borrow rate.

One deviation from the plan, made after Part A and the first rows of Part B had been seen
at the configured $2,500: every table below runs at 100 times that balance. The paper
account books borrow in whole cents at every accrual, which mis-charges positions of a
few hundred dollars and charges an hourly account nothing; see the docstring of
run_exp024.py. Part A is unchanged by it (the $2,500 table is kept at the end).

Verdicts under the plan's rules
- Part A. WIDE does not hold on F1 or F2: its drawdown is lower than LIVE's, but its
  Sharpe is 0.15 and 0.21 lower (the rule allowed 0.1) and it does not beat HALF. On
  2018-2019 a wider stop was a smaller bet, not a better one. The same-side caps lower
  drawdown and Sharpe together.
- Part B. Every setting with buffer 8 is positive at modelled costs on both fresh slices;
  buffer 8 has the higher Sharpe than buffer 4 at every collateral level on F3, F4,
  development and validation, so its neighbours agree. Collateral does not change Sharpe.
  The 6% cap does not bind at 1% collateral and gives mixed results above it. Selected:
  buffer 8 with the 5x ceiling, no cap; the collateral is the owner's choice.
- Part C. T1 (4h) has the best Sharpe on every slice. T2 (hourly decisions, 4h trend) is
  positive at modelled costs on every slice and lower than T1 on every slice; it adds 60%
  more entries and 8% more decisions. T3 (the rule on hourly candles) loses on every slice
  at modelled costs, so 15-minute candles were not tested.
- At twice the modelled costs the twenty-coin account is about flat (F3 +3%, F4 -3% to
  -7%, validation -3% to -7%).
- Live cross-check: main replayed from 2026-11-22 20:00 UTC opens the same three shorts at
  6.9x, 7.1x and 9.2x (live 7.0x, 7.1x, 9.2x), all three stop in the candle that closes
  2026-11-23 16:00 UTC, and all three re-open at 2026-11-24 00:00 UTC, as live did.
- Reference, same harness and files: main as configured on BTC, ETH, SOL opens 25.6 / 26.8
  entries a month, which are 18.9 / 18.7 decisions a month (development / validation).
