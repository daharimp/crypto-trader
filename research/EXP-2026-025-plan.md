# EXP-2026-025 plan: three add-ons for the top-ten hourly account

Written 2026-11-26 02:25 UTC and committed before any add-on was run. Research only; changes
no configuration. Simulated results on historical data are not a forecast; profit is not
guaranteed or implied.

## Why
Owner, 2026-11-26 02:16 UTC, on three ideas taken from open-source trading tools: "yes add
these bot improvements". They are to be backtested first and put into the new account
(top ten coins, hourly decisions) before any other:
1. pause a coin after repeated stop-outs, plus a cooldown after each loss (freqtrade's
   "Protections");
2. size each position by the coin's volatility and shrink it when coins move together
   (ai-hedge-fund's risk manager);
3. a time limit that closes trades going nowhere (Hummingbot's time barrier).
Every number that switches one of these on is a risk value and is the owner's to approve.

## Baseline (B0): the account the owner chose
Coins BTC, ETH, BNB, XRP, SOL, TRX, HYPE, ZEC, DOGE, LINK; one position a coin, long and
short; decisions, marks and stops every hour with the trend read from 4h candles (the live
signal, `trend_50_200_range_10_30`); collateral 1% of equity a position; stop at 30% of
collateral; take-profit ladder 4/8/12R closing 25/25/50%; liquidation buffer 8 daily moves;
leverage ceiling 5x; `max_open_positions` 10; costs 7 bps a side and 0.0016% an hour.
Run at 100 times the $2,500 balance so whole-cent borrow rounding does not distort it
(EXP-2026-024, `run_exp024.py`).

## What each add-on is, exactly
The account already has two related rules, which stay: after a stop-loss or the last
take-profit a coin opens nothing more until the next UTC day (ADR-038), and nothing opens
once the day's loss reaches 15%.

**1a. Cooldown after a losing trade (CD).** When a position closes for good and the whole
trade lost money after fees (opening fee, closing fees, borrow, take-profit proceeds all
counted), that coin opens nothing for C hours from the exit. A signal change still closes a
position at once; only the new open waits. C: 4 (one trend candle), 12, 24. Central: 12.

**1b. Stop guard (SG).** When a coin has had N opening stops (a stop before any
take-profit, i.e. a full planned loss) within the last L days, it opens nothing for P days
from the last of them. (N, L, P): (2, 7, 7) central; (2, 7, 3); (3, 14, 7).

**2. Correlation sizing (VC).** Volatility sizing is already how the account works: every
position risks the same 0.3% of equity at its stop and its leverage is 1 / (8 daily moves),
so nothing is added for volatility. The new part shrinks a new position's collateral by how
much it adds to bets already open. For a new position in coin m on side s, r is the mean,
over open positions j, of (+1 if j is on side s, else -1) x the correlation of m's and j's
4h returns over the last 180 completed 4h candles (30 days; fewer than 60 common candles
counts as 0). With no open position r is 0.
- VC-tiers (after ai-hedge-fund, without its increases above full size): multiplier 1.0
  when r < 0.4, 0.85 when 0.4 <= r < 0.6, 0.7 when 0.6 <= r < 0.8, 0.5 when r >= 0.8.
- VC-root: multiplier 1 / sqrt(1 + S), where S is the sum over open positions of the same
  signed correlation, floored at 0 (the size that keeps portfolio risk growing like that
  of unrelated bets), never below 0.25.
Each is compared with B0 and with "same size": B0 at a fixed collateral equal to 1% x the
variant's average multiplier, because any shrink lowers return and drawdown together.

**3. Time limit (TL).** At a decision, a position that has been open T hours or more, has
hit no take-profit, and is not in profit after fees at the decision price, is closed
("TIME_LIMIT"). That coin then opens nothing on the same side until its signal has been
flat or on the other side at least once; the other side may open at any time. T: 24, 72,
168. Central: 72.

## Measures, per variant and slice
Return a year, Sharpe (daily), max drawdown, worst day, entries a month, decision clusters
a month, how often the add-on acted (a month), fees a year; the same at twice the costs;
and the difference in Sharpe from B0 with a 90% interval from a paired block bootstrap of
daily returns (blocks of 10 days, 2,000 draws).

## Rule, fixed now
An add-on setting **helps** if its Sharpe is higher than B0's on at least three of the four
slices, is not more than 0.05 lower on any, and its two neighbours in the grid agree in
sign on at least three slices. It **does no harm** if it does not help but its Sharpe is
within 0.05 of B0's on every slice. Otherwise it **hurts**.
- "Helps": recommended to the owner at the central setting (or the passing neighbour).
- "Does no harm": reported as such; it goes in only if the owner still wants it.
- "Hurts": reported with the numbers and recommended against.
The settings that help are then run together once on every slice; the combination must
itself not lower Sharpe on more than one slice.
More trades was the owner's reason for this account, so entries a month are reported next
to every result and a setting that removes more than a quarter of the entries is flagged.
Nothing is re-tuned after the results are seen; anything added later is labelled
exploratory.

## What earlier experiments already say (so the result is not oversold)
EXP-2026-023 found that confirmation and minimum-hold rules did not beat the live signal on
4h, that profit comes from trades held longer than seven days, and that under a first-come
risk cap the entries that were cut down earned about twice as much per dollar as the ones
left whole. These three ideas come from tools built mostly for faster, mean-reverting
strategies, so the prior is that few will help a trend rule. The hourly account does have
one known weakness they could address: extra entries from re-reading the unfinished 4h
candle that close within hours at a small loss (EXP-2026-023, degen study).
