EXP-2026-026: does any confidence measure earn a size range?
================================================================================
Date: 2026-11-26. Research only; changes no configuration. Plan (committed before any
measure was computed, with one dated amendment): EXP-2026-026-plan.md. Run:
research/run_exp026.py; harness: research/exp026_lib.py. Simulated results on historical
data with modelled costs. Not a forecast; profit is not guaranteed or implied.

Account A is main as configured (SOL, ETH, BTC; 4h decisions; buffer 4; 30% stop; ladder);
account B is the top-ten hourly account (1% a position, buffer 8, 5x ceiling). Both at 100
times the $2,500 balance. Engine: the one merged with PR 69. A2 and A3 of step 2 were run
after the cooldown setting of ADR-053 was added to the engine (switched off); step 2 on A1
and both self-checks were repeated with that engine and are identical to the digit.

Measures (each 0 to 1, from candles that had closed at the decision):
  M1 breadth: share of the account's other coins whose signal is on the same side
  M2 trend strength: efficiency ratio of the coin's 4h closes, as a percentile of its own past
  M3 timeframe agreement: the 4h and the daily long averages both agree with the side
  M4 distance from the slow average, as a percentile of its own past (direction read on A1
     only: far is better)
  MC composite: the plain average of M1, M2 and M3
Slices: A1 2020-10 to 2023-12, A2 2024-01 to 2026-11, A3 BTC ETH LTC 2018-02 to 2019-12;
B1 to B4 are S1 to S4 of EXP-2026-025. Fresh slices (no period or no coin in common with
where the breadth hint was found): A3, B3, B4.

How the rules were applied (fixed in run_exp026.py before any score was computed): step 1
on account A is judged at 10% a position, the size the owner's amendment made the one that
would run; the 2% account is printed beside it. M4's direction was read on A1 at that size
and held everywhere else. A result is in R: the whole trade's profit or loss after all
fees, divided by the loss planned at the opening stop.

Verdict under the plan's rules
- Step 1, is the score informative (top third's mean result above the bottom third's on
  at least 5 of 7 slices, including 2 of the 3 fresh ones)?
  M1 breadth: YES, 7 of 7. M3 timeframe agreement: YES, 6 of 7 (2 of 3 fresh).
  MC composite: YES, 7 of 7. M2 trend strength: no, 4 of 7. M4 distance: no, 4 of 7.
- Step 2, does sizing by it beat a flat account of the same average size (wide range,
  Sharpe above the twin's on at least 5 of 7 including 2 fresh, narrow range agreeing in
  sign on at least 5)?
  MC composite: YES, 5 of 7 (2 of 3 fresh), narrow agrees on 6. Every other measure: no
  (M1 4 of 7, M3 4 of 7 with 1 fresh, M2 2 of 7, M4 1 of 7).
- So one measure, the composite, EARNS THE RANGE under the rule written beforehand, and by
  the plan it is the one put to the owner. It passes by the smallest margin the rule
  allows, and four things about that pass belong beside it:
  1. The gains are small and mostly inside the noise. Wide-range Sharpe differences:
     +0.03, -0.03, +0.01, +0.08, +0.05, +0.13, -0.06. Six of the seven 90% intervals
     include zero (only B3, the ten other majors, excludes it). The fresh-slice condition
     is met by A3 at +0.010 with an interval of -0.19 to +0.19.
  2. The size-free check does not pass. With account A at 2% a position the same rule
     gives 4 of 7 and 1 of 3 fresh (A1 +0.08, A2 -0.08, A3 -0.02). The pass depends on the
     size main happens to run at.
  3. The score does not pick winners; it picks bigger winners. The pooled rank correlation
     with the result is about zero for every measure (-0.06 to +0.01; MC -0.016, interval
     -0.036 to +0.004), and the top third wins about as often as the bottom third (32% to
     39% against 31% to 41%; more often on three slices, less often on four). What differs
     is the size of the wins: on main an average winner in the top third makes 1.9R to
     2.2R against 1.1R to 1.2R in the bottom third. On main that gap is still there
     without each third's best 1% of trades; on the hourly account half or more of it
     goes (exploratory table at the end). An edge that lives in the size of a few winners
     is real money when it shows up and slow to confirm.
  4. It concentrates risk. The composite is highest when the other coins point the same
     way, which is when the open positions move together. Against its twin of the same
     average size, the sized account's largest drop is deeper on all seven slices (by 1 to
     8 points) and its worst day is worse on six: on main -23.8% against -17.7% (A1),
     -26.5% against -18.8% (A2), -22.2% against -17.7% (A3); on the hourly account
     -3.2% to -5.5% against -2.2% to -5.0%. Return a year is higher on six of seven and
     return over drawdown on five of seven. At twice the costs the Sharpe difference is
     positive on six of seven (+0.05 to +0.20; B4 -0.01).
- The evidence is firmer on the ten-coin hourly account than on main. On B the composite
  is ahead on three of four slices (+0.08, +0.05, +0.13, -0.06); on main it is +0.03,
  -0.03, +0.01, a coin flip. With three coins breadth has only three values; with ten it
  has ten.
- M1 (breadth alone) and M3 (timeframe agreement alone) are informative but do not pass
  step 2. M3 is the best single measure on A1 and B1 (+0.14, +0.25) and clearly negative
  on A2, A3 and B4 (-0.12, -0.19, -0.17): it is not stable. M4 hurts as a size rule
  (lower on six of seven).

What follows (the plan: the composite is recommended to the owner with the range the owner
has chosen). The range and where it runs are the owner's decisions; nothing here changes a
setting. Suggested order, given how narrow the pass is: build the range as a per-account
setting; switch the composite on in the ten-coin paper account first (0.5% to 1.5% a
position around its 1%); keep main at a flat 10% inside its 5% to 15% range until the
paper trades show the same pattern, because main is where the evidence is weakest and the
worst day grows most. Every entry would log its score, so the comparison with flat sizing
can be made from the same trades.

optional setting (risk.min_collateral_per_position_pct with the score from

score from the last 719 completed candles only, which is what the exchange's feed
