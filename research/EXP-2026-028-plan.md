# EXP-2026-028 plan: do the cooldown and the size range still help when both are on?

Written 2026-11-26 07:36 UTC and committed before any run with both rules on. Research only;
changes no configuration. Simulated results on historical data are not a forecast; profit
is not guaranteed or implied.

## Why
Two owner choices are open for the ten-coin hourly paper account: the length of a cooldown
after a losing trade (UD-34) and whether a confidence score picks each trade's size inside
a range (UD-35). Each was tested alone and each was recommended for this account:
- EXP-2026-025: the cooldown, at a fixed 1% a position, raised Sharpe on every slice at 4
  and 24 hours and on three of four at 12, mostly by not paying fees on quick re-entries
  after a small loss. It removes 26% to 46% of the entries.
- EXP-2026-026: the composite score, with no cooldown, sizing inside 0.5% to 1.5%, beat a
  flat account of the same average size on three of the four slices of this account
  (+0.08, +0.05, +0.13, -0.06). Its edge is bigger winners in the high-score trades, not
  more winners.
They have never been run together. The cooldown changes which entries exist, so the
score's small edge may or may not survive it, and the range changes the size of the trades
whose fees the cooldown saves. Both built settings are on main (ADR-053, ADR-054) and can
be switched on in one settings change, so this is checked first.

## Account and slices
Account B of EXP-2026-026 (B0 of EXP-2026-025): the ten-coin hourly account as it runs (1%
of equity a position, 30% stop, 4/8/12R ladder, buffer 8, 5x ceiling, main's costs), at
100 times the $2,500 balance, on the same four slices: B1 (the ten coins, 2020-10 to
2023-12), B2 (the ten coins, 2024-01 to 2026-11), B3 (ten other majors, 2020-10 to
2026-11), B4 (ten majors of 2018, 2018-08 to 2019-12). B3 and B4 are the fresh slices of
EXP-2026-026. Main's account (4h, three coins) is not part of this: neither rule is
proposed for it now.

## Runs, per slice
Both rules run as built into the paper account (`risk.cooldown_after_loss_hours`,
`risk.min_collateral_per_position_pct` with `trader.perps.confidence`), not as research
copies. Range: 0.5% to 1.5%, the one put to the owner. Cooldown h: 4 hours (the length
recommended; the one judged), and 12 and 24 hours (the owner's other options).
- N: neither (1% flat).
- C_h: cooldown only (1% flat).
- R: range only.
- CR_h: both.
- T_h: the flat twin of CR_h: the same cooldown at a fixed size equal to CR_h's average
  collateral % over its entries, so only the placement of size differs.
Reported for each: return a year, Sharpe (daily), largest drop, worst day, return over
drop, entries a month; CR_4, T_4, R and C_4 also at twice the costs. Sharpe differences
carry a 90% interval from a paired block bootstrap of daily returns (10-day blocks, 2,000
draws), as in EXP-2026-026.

## Already seen when this was written
Every N, C_h and R number above (they are rows of EXP-2026-025 and EXP-2026-026). No run
with both rules on has been made on any slice.

## Rules, fixed now
1. **The range still earns its place beside the cooldown** if Sharpe(CR_4) is above
   Sharpe(T_4) on at least three of the four slices, including at least one of the two
   fresh ones. (The proportion of the EXP-2026-026 rule: five of seven, two of three
   fresh.)
2. **The cooldown still helps beside the range** if Sharpe(CR_4) is above Sharpe(R) on at
   least three of the four slices and not more than 0.05 lower on any. (The EXP-2026-025
   rule. R and CR_4 differ slightly in average size; at these sizes Sharpe barely depends
   on it, and the average sizes are printed.)
The 12-hour and 24-hour rows are judged by the same two rules, each length on its own, and
reported for the owner's other options. They do not change the 4-hour verdict.

## What follows from the result
- Both rules hold: the recommendation stands (cooldown and range together in the ten-coin
  account).
- Rule 1 fails, rule 2 holds: the cooldown is recommended alone. For sizing, "record the
  score, keep sizes flat" (smallest = largest), so every paper trade carries its score and
  the score can be judged on real fills before it moves size.
- Rule 1 holds, rule 2 fails: the range is recommended alone; the cooldown would cost
  entries without adding to it.
- Both fail: the two do not stack. The cooldown, whose evidence alone is the firmer of the
  two, is recommended alone, with the score recorded at a flat size.
The owner is told the result before either setting is switched on, whatever it is. Nothing
is re-tuned after the results are seen; anything beyond the runs above is labelled
exploratory.
