EXP-2026-028: do the cooldown and the size range still help when both are on?
================================================================================
Date: 2026-11-26. Research only; changes no configuration. Plan (committed and pushed
before any run with both rules on): EXP-2026-028-plan.md. Run: research/run_exp028.py.
Simulated results on historical data with modelled costs. Not a forecast; profit is not
guaranteed or implied.

The account is the ten-coin hourly paper account as it runs (1% of equity posted per
position, 30% stop, 4/8/12R ladder, buffer 8, 5x ceiling, main's costs), at 100 times its
$2,500 balance, on the four slices of EXP-2026-025 and EXP-2026-026:
  B1  BTC ETH BNB XRP SOL TRX HYPE ZEC DOGE LINK, 2020-10-01 to 2024-01-01
  B2  the same ten coins, 2024-01-01 to 2026-11-25
  B3  LTC BCH ADA DOT XLM XMR EOS XTZ THETA UNI, 2020-10-01 to 2026-11-25 (fresh)
  B4  BTC ETH LTC BNB XRP ADA TRX XLM EOS NEO, 2018-08-01 to 2020-01-01 (fresh)
Engine: the one merged with PR 71. Both rules run as built into the paper account
(risk.cooldown_after_loss_hours of ADR-053; risk.min_collateral_per_position_pct with the
score of trader.perps.confidence, ADR-054), not as research copies. Range 0.5% to 1.5%.

Runs per slice: N neither (1% flat, the account as it runs today); C_h cooldown only; R
range only; CR_h both; T_h the flat twin of CR_h (the same cooldown at one fixed size
equal to CR_h's average size, so only the placement of size differs). h is 4 hours (the
length recommended, the one judged), and 12 and 24 hours (the owner's other options).

Verdict under the plan's rules
- Rule 1, does the range still earn its place beside the cooldown (Sharpe of CR_4 above
  T_4 on at least three of four slices, including one of the two fresh ones)?
  YES: +0.072, +0.025, +0.097, -0.033. Three of four; of the fresh slices B3 is above
  and B4 is below.
- Rule 2, does the cooldown still help beside the range (Sharpe of CR_4 above R on at
  least three of four slices and not more than 0.05 lower on any)?
  YES: +0.109, +0.164, +0.115, +0.056. Four of four.
- The other lengths, each judged on its own by the same two rules: both hold at 12 hours
  (rule 1 on four of four, rule 2 on four of four) and at 24 hours (rule 1 on three of
  four with B4 below, rule 2 on four of four).
- So by the plan the recommendation stands: the cooldown and the range together in the
  ten-coin paper account. Whether either is switched on, and the cooldown's length, are
  the owner's decisions (UD-34, UD-35); nothing here changes a setting.

What belongs beside that verdict
1. The two do not get in each other's way, and they do not multiply either. Added up,
   the cooldown's gain alone and the range's gain alone come to about what the two give
   together: at 4 hours the combined change in Sharpe against the account as it runs is
   +0.187, +0.213, +0.244, -0.002, and the sum of the two separate changes is +0.194,
   +0.237, +0.276, -0.025 (table near the end). The range changes no trade, only its
   size: the account with both makes the same number of entries and starts the same
   number of cooldowns as the cooldown alone on every slice at every length, and compared
   event by event on B2 and B4 at 4 and 24 hours the two logs agree in time, kind, coin,
   side and reason throughout (exploratory; that script is kept with the run outputs, not
   in the repository).
2. The cooldown is the larger and steadier of the two. Beside the range it adds +0.06 to
   +0.16 at 4 hours (+0.03 to +0.30 at 12, +0.06 to +0.39 at 24), and at 4 hours its 90%
   interval excludes zero on B2 and B3. The range adds about what it added alone (alone,
   EXP-2026-026: +0.08, +0.05, +0.13, -0.06): small, and still mostly inside the noise.
   At 4 hours three of the four intervals include zero and the fourth (B3) starts at
   +0.004. The fresh-slice condition is met by B3 only; on the 2018-2019 slice the range
   lowers Sharpe at 4 and 24 hours.
3. The range still deepens the falls, cooldown or not. Against its twin of the same
   average size, the account with both has a deeper largest drop on all four slices
   (19.1% against 16.3%, 13.2% against 12.6%, 26.7% against 23.5%, 13.0% against 11.4%)
   and a worse worst day on all four (-3.7% against -3.6%, -3.2% against -2.2%, -5.4%
   against -5.0%, -4.0% against -3.9%). Its return a year is higher on all four (20.6
   against 16.6, 13.1 against 11.3, 7.6 against 5.3, 14.9 against 13.6) and its return
   over largest drop on three.
4. Against the account as it runs today (N), both together: return a year 20.6% against
   15.9%, 13.1% against 9.3%, 7.6% against 3.4%, 14.9% against 14.0%; Sharpe 1.38 against
   1.19, 0.99 against 0.78, 0.55 against 0.30, 0.93 against 0.93; largest drop 19.1%
   against 17.9%, 13.2% against 15.1%, 26.7% against 23.6%, 13.0% against 11.8%. Higher
   return on all four slices, higher Sharpe on three and level on the fourth, a deeper
   largest drop on three.
5. It costs trades, and the owner asked for more of them. The cooldown removes 26% of the
   entries at 4 hours on every slice (116.9 to 86.9 a month on B1), 37% to 39% at 12 and
   46% to 47% at 24, the same with the range as without. With both on, Sharpe rises with
   the length on three slices (4 / 12 / 24 hours: B1 1.38 / 1.40 / 1.45, B2 0.99 / 1.13 /
   1.22, B3 0.55 / 0.56 / 0.64) and not on B4 (0.93 / 0.90 / 0.93). Four hours keeps the
   most trades for the paper data; a longer wait buys a higher simulated Sharpe with fewer
   trades. That trade-off is the owner's (UD-34).
6. At twice the costs the picture holds. Both against the twin: +0.134, +0.107, +0.165,
   +0.016; both against range only: +0.188, +0.302, +0.206, +0.178. With both on at twice
   the costs the account makes 11.2%, 3.8%, -1.3% and 6.9% a year (Sharpe 0.81, 0.35,
   -0.01, 0.49), against 5.3%, -2.0%, -6.8% and 3.8% (0.46, -0.10, -0.42, 0.32) for the
   account as it runs (those four from EXP-2026-025). The cooldown starts a little more
   often there (2,140 times against 2,053 on B1), which is what doubled fees do to trades
   that end near zero.
